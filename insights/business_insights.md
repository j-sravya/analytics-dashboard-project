# Business Insights

## Sales Insights

- Total revenue from the synthetic dataset is Rs. 19,236,966.82 across 1,500 orders.
- Electronics is the strongest revenue category, supported by high-value products such as laptops and smartphones.
- Fashion also performs strongly because it combines frequent purchases with moderate order values.
- Monthly sales show stronger activity during festive and year-end periods, especially around September to December.

## Customer Behavior Insights

- The Consumer segment generates the highest number of orders, making it the main audience for acquisition and retention campaigns.
- Corporate and Small Business customers place fewer orders but can be valuable for bulk purchase campaigns and loyalty programs.
- UPI is the most used payment method, showing that fast digital payments should remain a checkout priority.

## Operational Recommendations

- Bengaluru and Hyderabad are high-performing cities and should receive priority in regional promotions and faster inventory planning.
- Standard shipping is the most common shipping option, but Express and Same Day delivery can be promoted for high-margin categories.
- Average delivery time is about 4.7 days, which can be reduced by improving fulfillment in top sales cities.

## Product Recommendations

- Continue promoting Electronics as the main revenue driver, especially laptops, smartphones, and wearables.
- Use cross-selling strategies for Accessories with Electronics orders.
- Improve visibility for Beauty and Grocery products through bundles, discounts, and repeat-purchase campaigns.

## Growth Suggestions

- Launch city-specific festive campaigns for Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Delhi.
- Create segment-based offers: cashback for Consumers, invoice-friendly checkout for Corporate customers, and bulk discounts for Small Business customers.
- Track monthly revenue, profit margin, average order value, and delivery days as core dashboard KPIs.
- Build product recommendation models in future versions using customer segment, category, city, and payment behavior.
