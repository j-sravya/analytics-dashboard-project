# Power BI Dashboard Layout Concept

## Page 1: Executive Overview

- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Top Category, Best City, Most Used Payment Method
- Line chart: Monthly Sales Trend
- Bar chart: Revenue by Category
- Bar chart: Top Cities by Sales
- Slicers: Month, Product Category, City, Customer Segment

## Page 2: Sales Analysis

- Monthly revenue and profit trend
- Revenue by category
- Profit by category
- Average order value by month
- City and state level sales comparison

## Page 3: Customer Insights

- Customer segment distribution
- Segment-wise revenue
- Payment method usage
- Shipping type preference
- Average delivery days by shipping type

## Page 4: Product Performance

- Top 10 products by revenue
- Units sold by product
- Category profit margin
- Product category contribution to total sales

## Dashboard Design Notes

- Use a clean white background with navy section headers.
- Use blue and teal accents for revenue and profit visuals.
- Keep KPI cards at the top of each page for quick recruiter scanning.
- Add slicers for month, city, product category, and customer segment.
